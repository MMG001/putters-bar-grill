Chapter 01

History of Verona WI

Verona traces its roots to the 1840s, when settlers from the East arrived on the rolling prairie of what would become southwestern Dane County. The community took its name from Verona, New York, the hometown of early settlers, and grew as a farming crossroads along the military road between Madison and the lead-mining region to the southwest. Verona incorporated as a village in 1921 and achieved city status in 1978.

For nearly a century, the area was also home to the Dane County Asylum and poor farm on the land that is now Badger Prairie County Park — the institution opened in 1882 and closed in 1973. Verona's modern chapter began in 2005, when Epic Systems moved its world headquarters from Madison to a sprawling campus on Verona's west side, transforming the quiet farm town nicknamed "Hometown U.S.A." into one of Wisconsin's fastest-growing cities. Today Verona balances small-town traditions like the annual Hometown Days festival with the energy of a booming tech-corridor community.

Verona's economy is anchored by one of the most famous corporate campuses in America, alongside a healthy mix of local manufacturers, healthcare, and small business along the Verona Road (US 18/151) corridor.

Chapter 06

History of Wisconsin

Wisconsin's name derives from the Wisconsin River, recorded by French explorers from an Algonquian-language word. Jacques Marquette and Louis Jolliet traveled the river in 1673, opening the region to the fur trade. The Wisconsin Territory was organized on July 3, 1836, with Madison chosen as its capital, and Wisconsin entered the Union as the 30th state on May 29, 1848 — the same decade Verona's first settlers arrived.

The state grew on lead mining (earning the "Badger State" nickname from miners who burrowed into hillsides), lumber, and dairy farming that made Wisconsin "America's Dairyland." That heritage lives on in Verona — from the Military Ridge corridor that once carried settlers and soldiers, to the cheese curds on every good bar menu in Dane County, including ours.

Transportation & Public Transit

  • US Highways 18/151 (Verona Road) — Freeway connection to Madison's Beltline (US 12/14), about 15 minutes to downtown Madison.
  • Madison Metro Transit — Commuter bus service connecting Verona and the Epic Systems campus with Madison. Customer service: (608) 266-4466 · mymetrobus.com
  • Dane County Regional Airport (MSN) — 4000 International Ln, Madison, WI 53704. Full-service commercial airport about 25 minutes from Verona.
  • Military Ridge State Trail — Trailhead near downtown Verona. A 40-mile crushed-limestone rail-trail running from the Madison area west to Dodgeville for biking, running, and snowmobiling.
  • Ice Age National Scenic Trail — Verona Segment — A signature stretch of Wisconsin's thousand-mile national scenic trail passes right through the city.
