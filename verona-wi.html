Chapter 01

History of Verona WI

Verona's story begins at Badger Mill. After the land was ceded to the U.S. government in 1829 and 1832, early settlement centered on the mill site, whose grist mill — completed in 1844 — is described by the city as Dane County's first. Samuel Taylor later added a store and post office, forming an early commercial center. The Wisconsin Territorial Legislature established the Town of Verona on February 11, 1847, and the community took its name from Verona, New York, hometown of early settlers George and William Vroman.

The Village of Verona incorporated on January 22, 1921. In 1966 the community earned its enduring nickname when a detachment of American servicemen fighting in Vietnam identified Verona as their ideal example of "Hometown U.S.A." The City of Verona was established on April 18, 1978, partly amid concern about being absorbed into Madison's municipal orbit. Verona's modern chapter began when Epic Systems moved its world headquarters to the city's west side, transforming the farm town into one of Wisconsin's fastest-growing cities — while traditions like the Hometown Days festival keep the hometown in Hometown U.S.A.

  • 1829 & 1832 — Land cessions to the U.S. government precede organized settlement.
  • 1844 — Badger Mill completed; Dane County's first grist mill.
  • February 11, 1847 — Town of Verona established by the Wisconsin Territorial Legislature; named for Verona, New York.
  • January 22, 1921 — Village of Verona incorporated by referendum.
  • 1966 — "Hometown U.S.A." nickname adopted.
  • April 18, 1978 — City of Verona established.

Chapter 06

History of Wisconsin

Wisconsin's name derives from the Wisconsin River, recorded by French explorers from an Algonquian-language word. Jacques Marquette and Louis Jolliet traveled the river in 1673, opening the region to the fur trade. The Wisconsin Territory was organized on July 3, 1836, with Madison chosen as its capital, and Wisconsin entered the Union as the 30th state on May 29, 1848 — the same decade Verona's first settlers arrived.

The state grew on lead mining (earning the "Badger State" nickname from miners who burrowed into hillsides), lumber, and dairy farming that made Wisconsin "America's Dairyland." That heritage lives on in Verona — from the Military Ridge corridor that once carried settlers and soldiers, to the cheese curds on every good bar menu in Dane County, including ours.

Parks & Outdoors

The City of Verona maintains 25 parks and four conservancy and special-use areas totaling more than 160 acres, alongside major Dane County and state trail resources.

  • Badger Prairie County Park — 4654 Maple Grove Dr, Verona, WI 53593. A 317-acre Dane County park with the Ice Age Trail Junction Area, dog parks, play fields, mountain-bike facilities, and shelters.
  • Military Ridge State Trail — Verona trail access near Epic Ln. Long-distance hiking and biking trail connecting the Madison area to Dodgeville.
  • Ice Age National Scenic Trail — Junction Area — Connected through Badger Prairie County Park; a signature stretch of Wisconsin's thousand-mile trail.
  • Prairie Moraine Dog Park — Dane County park system near Verona; a popular off-leash recreation area.
  • Fireman's Park & Hometown Junction Park — Downtown-area favorites; Fireman's Park hosts the annual Hometown Days festival, and Hometown Junction's depot theme anchors the Military Ridge trailhead.
  • Verona Parks & Urban Forestry — 410 Investment Ct, Verona, WI 53593 · (608) 848-6809 — the municipal contact for park reservations and recreation.
