Privacy policy

How we handle your information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this website, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) is sent to us by email so we can respond to your inquiry. We do not require you to create an account to use this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to questions, table bookings, and private event inquiries
  • To operate and improve our website

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

This website uses third-party services that may collect standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type), including Google Maps for our embedded location map and Google Fonts for typography. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content (such as the map) may use cookies according to the provider's policies.

Data Retention

Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and for reasonable business record-keeping.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request its deletion.
