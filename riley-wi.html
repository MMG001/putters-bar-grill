The Neighborhood

About Riley, Wisconsin

Riley is barely a dot on the Dane County map — a Town of Springdale crossroads named for the Riley brothers, who owned the land when the railroad came through, with a post office that served the settlement from 1882 to 1940. But every Military Ridge Trail rider knows it: Riley is the classic first waypoint on the grade west out of Verona.

The trail between Riley and our end of town runs through Sugar River headwaters country — five miles so flat and fast that Riley functionally counts as a Verona neighborhood with better birdwatching.
